## About the project

The **RDM Information Technology Portal** is an internal company website. Employees use it to reach documents and sites for **induction and training**, as well as **external sites connected to the company**.

It is built with **Python Flask**, **Microsoft SQL Server**, **HTML**, **CSS** and **JavaScript**. The application is in development and **waiting for sign-off before going live**.

## The four e-portfolio steps

- **Collect:** I gathered my charter, stories, backlog, sprint plans and logbook entries from the placement.
- **Select:** I chose the artefacts that best show how I plan and deliver work in an Agile way.
- **Reflect:** Each page ends with a structured reflection on what I learned.
- **Connect:** The charter sets the rules, the stories and backlog define the work, the sprint backlog plans it, and the logbook records what happened. The [connecting reflection](logbook.html#connecting-the-artefacts) ties all five together.
